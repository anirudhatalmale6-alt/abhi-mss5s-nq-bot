============================================================
 NQ 8-EMA REVERSAL BOT - HOW IT WORKS (plain English)
 The "Rutvi" setup, now also running on Abhi
 2026-07-28
============================================================

THE BIG IDEA
------------
The bot trades REVERSALS of the trend using the 8 EMA. It waits for a
trend to start weakening (two failed pushes) and then enters in the new
direction, aiming for a 1:1 risk/reward.

WHAT IT WATCHES
---------------
- Price is turned into 2-point Renko bricks (noise-filtered building
  blocks). It tracks an 8 EMA over those bricks.
- It also watches the 1-hour trend (8 EMA vs 21 EMA on the 1h chart).

THE SHORT SETUP (uptrend rolling over)
--------------------------------------
1. Price is riding above the 8 EMA in an uptrend.
2. Price breaks DOWN through the 8 EMA. That counts as break #1 - but it
   only "counts" if the high just before it was a LOWER high than the
   previous push up (the trend is losing steam).
3. Price bounces, then breaks down through the 8 EMA a SECOND time off
   another lower high. That is the trigger -> ENTER SHORT at market.

THE LONG SETUP (downtrend bottoming) - the mirror image
-------------------------------------------------------
Two break-UPS through the 8 EMA, each off a HIGHER low -> ENTER LONG.

THE 1-HOUR TREND GATE (this is the key filter)
----------------------------------------------
- A SHORT is only allowed if the 1-hour trend is RED (down).
- A LONG is only allowed if the 1-hour trend is GREEN (up).
- If the 1-hour is unclear, it skips the trade.
So it never fights the bigger-picture direction - the reversal has to
agree with the 1h.

STOP AND TARGET
---------------
- Stop loss: placed just past the swing that made the signal - 2 points
  beyond the last lower-high (for shorts) or last higher-low (for longs).
- Take profit: 1:1 - the target is the same distance away as the stop.
  So if the stop is 12 points away, the target is 12 points of profit.

ONE TRADE PER SETUP
-------------------
Each two-break pattern only fires a single entry. It won't spam the same
setup over and over.

STARTUP SAFETY
--------------
When the bot restarts, it replays the day's price history to rebuild the
EMA and the swing memory, but it does NOT fire any of those old/past
setups - it only goes "live" from the moment startup finishes. (This is
the piece I just fixed on Rutvi so it reliably takes the entry.)

WHEN IT DOES NOTHING
--------------------
If there is no valid two-break reversal that agrees with the 1h trend,
the bot simply sits flat and waits. No trade is normal and expected.

ABHI vs RUTVI
-------------

account it trades on. Same 8 EMA, same 2-point bricks, same 1h gate,
same 1:1 risk/reward.
